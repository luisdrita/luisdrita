# Luís Rita

### AI for clinical research and safer cities

I develop and evaluate machine-learning systems that turn complex clinical and urban data into useful evidence.

I'm a **Research Associate in Artificial Intelligence at Imperial College London**, **CEO and Co-founder of [CycleAI](https://www.cycleai.net/)**, and **Invited Professor and Scientific Coordinator in Cybersecurity at [Universidade Europeia](https://www.europeia.pt/faculty/professor-luis-rita-tecnologias/)**.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/luisdrita/) · [Publications / ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6789-6356) · [CycleAI on GitHub](https://github.com/CycleAI)

## What I work on

- **Clinical AI:** large language models for endoscopy and pathology reports, computer vision for medical images, and machine learning for cancer and epilepsy research. I focus on patient-level validation, reproducible analysis and preventing data leakage.
- **Safer mobility:** street-level imagery, crowdsourced perceptions, cycling-safety mapping and route planning through CycleAI, with research collaborations involving LSE and CITTA.
- **Computational medicine:** graph and network machine learning, molecule discovery and computational nutrition, building on my PhD at Imperial College London.
